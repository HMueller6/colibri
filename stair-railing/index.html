Sheet

Stair

The top landing is the last tread, so a stair has one tread fewer than risers. Width is clear width, wall face to wall face or to the inside of the guard.

Railing

Stair rail height is measured up from the nosing line, landing guard height from the landing floor. The guard continues along the landing on its side.

Summary

Material list

Rails are cut to fit between posts, measured along the slope. Pickets are measured at their centre, from the tread or landing to the underside of the top rail. Posts are the length drawn; add what your anchor needs. Stock is the next length up: rails 8' to 16' (spliced in equal pieces when longer), stringers up to 24'.
